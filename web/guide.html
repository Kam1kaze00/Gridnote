Gridnote Guide

Open a notebook or PDF, or choose New Notebook for fresh paper. Gridnote remembers where you stopped.

Writing and navigation

Q Brush · W Selector · E Eraser · R Text. Tap a tool for its options. Choose Pen for subtle pressure, Brush for wider variation, or Fineliner for a constant width. Turn off pen pressure for a steady line with any tip.

On a two-switch tablet, tap the upper switch, erase, then lift to return to Brush. Hold the lower switch and move vertically to scroll. On touchscreens, pinch to zoom. Writing controls include finger writing and scroll speed where supported.

Regular erases part of a stroke. Stroke removes a whole mark. Rectangle and Lasso erase groups of strokes. Undo restores an erasing gesture.

Organizing a page

Paste a screenshot or use Add image. On a phone or tablet, choose Take photo and crop it. Selector moves objects and snaps them to nearby edges. Drag a selection corner to resize. Use Table to edit a cell. Tap the paper control to change the ruling or tint.

Working through an exercise

Choose Solve and tap a highlighted item. Compare the reading with its image and edit unclear symbols. Try 5x + 4 = 3, 5 · 4 or 72 km/h to m/s. General word problems, diagrams and longer code can use the optional AI service. Ask AI sends the displayed text and image. Copy to ChatGPT lets you paste them yourself.

Pages and subjects

Open Pages in the lower left to jump to a thumbnail. Select a page and use the plus, move or delete buttons. Undo restores the full page and its annotations. In the notebook menu (Edit on Mac), Remove Typed Text lets you clean this page or all pages. Filled tables and other objects are kept.

On startup, choose your major and preview its six tools. Leave Major set to None for the regular notebook. Continue notebook restores your work, zoom and position. New notebook starts a fresh page. Each subject tool has a button below the writing toolbar. Tap one, adjust its inputs and use Insert to place a result on the page. To change your major, reopen Notebook Launcher from File on Mac or the notebook menu on the web.

Appearance and paper

Choose Theme on the launcher or open Options in the writing toolbar. Default opens on first use. Changing the theme updates the launcher artwork and controls immediately. The launcher remembers your choice. Default uses a pen nib and 70s Cartoon uses the pencil mascot. New cartoon notebooks start on ivory grid paper with a double margin and printed page numbers. In an existing notebook, choose Paper → 70s Cartoon paper for a page or the notebook default. Paper settings travel with the editable notebook and PDF.

Saving and sharing

A saved indicator reports local writes. The notebook menu can save an editable .gridnote copy. Preview PDF shows exactly which A4 pages will export. Use Share for the system share sheet or save the PDF and attach it yourself.

Taking a tablet to class

On iPad or iPhone, open Gridnote in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen and enable Open as Web App if shown. On Android, choose Install in the Chrome menu. Open Gridnote from its icon while online. When the launcher or notebook menu says Ready offline, you can disconnect. Use the same icon to return to your local notebooks.

Download an editable copy before clearing browser storage, switching browsers or moving to a new device. To report a problem, include your device, operating system, Gridnote version and the steps to reproduce it. Gridnote issues

Restoring settings

Restore tool defaults resets the current tool. Restore all settings in Writing controls resets tool preferences, the palette and device adjustments. Your pages, files and position are stored separately.
