Gridnote Guide

Open a notebook or PDF, or choose New Notebook for fresh paper. Gridnote remembers where you stopped.

Writing and navigation

Q Writer · W Selector · E Eraser · R Text. Tap a tool for its options. Pen lays down a lighter ballpoint line with a light touch and darkens as you press. Choose Width for the size of the tip and Ink flow for a lighter or darker Pen stroke. The preview and your next stroke use the new settings. Fountain gives broad and fine strokes as you change direction. Choose Brush, then Inked for a smooth, full downstroke and fine tip, or Dry for broken bristle marks on quick strokes. Gridnote remembers your choice. Pencil leaves a light paper grain and darkens as you shade over a mark. Fineliner keeps an even width through corners and dots. Each tip remembers its width. Soft pressure reaches thicker lines with less force. Firm gives you more room for light strokes. Turn off pressure for a steady line at the selected width. With a mouse or finger, Brush uses stroke speed to vary its width.

On a two-switch tablet, tap the upper switch, erase, then lift to return to Writer. Hold the lower switch and move vertically to scroll. On touchscreens, pinch to zoom. Writing controls include finger writing and scroll speed where supported.

Choose Shapes → X–Y axes and drag between opposite corners of your graph. The axes meet in the center, with equal tick spacing in both directions. Selector moves or resizes them together.

Regular erases part of a stroke. Stroke removes a whole mark. Rectangle and Lasso erase groups of strokes. Undo restores an erasing gesture.

Organizing a page

Paste a screenshot or use Add image. On a phone or tablet, choose Take photo and crop it. Selector moves objects and snaps them to nearby edges. Drag a selection corner to resize. Tap text or a cell with Selector to edit it. Tap the paper control to change the ruling or tint.

Text and tables

In Table, choose the number of columns and rows on the grid to insert a table. You can also enter exact dimensions or drag its footprint on the page. Table options change the alignment, color, size and emphasis across the table. Open Text while editing a cell or note to format it individually. On touchscreens use the arrows to move between cells and Done to finish typing. Tab and Shift+Tab also move between cells.

Working through an exercise

Choose Solve. A single exercise opens immediately. If the page has several, tap a highlighted item. Compare the reading with its image and edit unclear symbols. Try 5x + 4 = 3, 5 · 4 or 72 km/h to m/s. For general word problems, diagrams and longer code, choose Ask AI in a desktop app. On first use, approve Gridnote in your browser. Gridnote sends the displayed exercise and crop using your ChatGPT plan and shows the answer here. Use Account to manage usage or disconnect. Copy puts the exercise on your clipboard.

Managing pages

Open Pages in the lower left to jump to a thumbnail. Select a page and use the plus, move or delete buttons. Undo restores the full page and its annotations. Tap Clear Writing beside the paper control to remove Writer strokes from the current page. Typed notes, highlights, shapes, images, tables and code blocks stay in place. Undo restores the handwriting. To remove typed notes on one page or all pages, use Remove Typed Text in the notebook menu (Edit on Mac).

Paper

Open the paper control to choose grid, ruled, dotted or whiteboard paper and a color. Apply the change to this page or use it as the notebook default. Your PDF uses the same paper.

New photos and screenshots start at their natural size up to 15 cm wide and 10 cm tall. They shrink further to fit the visible writing area. The original pixels are retained for zooming and PDF export. To use more of the page, choose Selection → Fit Image to Page. Drag a corner to see millimetres and matching widths or heights when alignment guides are on. Undo restores the previous size.

Saving and sharing

A saved indicator reports local writes. The notebook menu can save an editable .gridnote copy. Preview PDF shows exactly which A4 pages will export. Use Share for the system share sheet or save the PDF and attach it yourself.

Taking a tablet to class

The iPad app is ready offline on first launch. Use Share or save PDF to send a copy or save it to Files. To install the web version on iPad or iPhone, open Gridnote in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen and enable Open as Web App if shown. On Android, choose Install in the Chrome menu. Open Gridnote from its icon while online. When the launcher or notebook menu says Ready offline, you can disconnect. Use the same icon to return to your local notebooks.

Download an editable copy before clearing browser storage, switching browsers or moving to a new device. To report a problem, include your device, operating system, Gridnote version and the steps to reproduce it. Gridnote issues

Restoring settings

Restore tool defaults resets the current tool. Restore all settings in Writing controls resets tool preferences, the palette and device adjustments. Your pages, files and position are stored separately.

Gridnote for iPad privacy policy

The iPad app stores notebooks, camera photos, imported files and writing settings on your device. Handwriting, image recognition and PDF export run there. Gridnote has no account, advertising or analytics and does not collect or track your data.

The camera is used when you choose to photograph an exercise. Saving to Files or sharing sends a copy to the location or app you choose. Its storage and privacy settings then apply. You can remove the app and its local notebooks from iPad Settings. Copies you have exported can be deleted from the destination you chose. For privacy questions, visit Gridnote support.
